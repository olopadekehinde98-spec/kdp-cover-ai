📸 How to export this as your YouTube banner

  1. Open this file in Chrome (double-click youtube-banner.html)
  2. Press Win + Shift + S → select the banner area exactly
  3. Paste into Paint or Snipping Tool → Save as PNG
  4. Resize to 2560×1440 in any free tool: canva.com → resize → upload image → download
  5. In YouTube Studio → Customization → Branding → Banner image → Upload

The dashed white rectangle shows YouTube's safe zone — everything inside is visible on all devices (TV, desktop, mobile). Your logo and tagline are centred inside it.
